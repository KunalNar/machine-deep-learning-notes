Why Density-Based (Generative) Models Fail:
To estimate density p(x|C), data points must populate all k^D cells. In 1D, 25 points pack the segments. In 3D, >80% of volume is empty. In 10D (5^10 ≈ 10 million cells), 25 points leave 99.999% of space completely void. Linear Classifiers (Discriminative) solve this by drawing a hyperplane w^T x + w_0 = 0 directly, requiring only O(D) parameters!
